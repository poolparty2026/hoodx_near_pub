1What Poolside on NEAR is

Poolside is an autonomous liquidity agent for Rhea pools on NEAR. A creator writes a strategy as a set of rules, and the agent runs it from a strategy wallet: it sets ranges, rebalances, collects fees and closes positions. Followers can join from any chain through NEAR Intents.

The rules stay private while the strategy runs, but its profit and loss is public. A follower can buy a viewing key to see the rules early, and at close anyone can check that the agent kept to them.

2How it works

1CreateThe creator writes rules in a fixed format: pool, range width, rebalance trigger, take profit, stop loss and close. Their hash goes on-chain.
2FollowA follower buys a viewing key to see the rules and position while the strategy runs, then joins it or mirrors it from their own wallet.
3RunThe agent acts through a key that can only call the strategy contract. The contract refuses any action outside the public limits.
4Close and checkThe creator closes, the fee split settles on a NEAR contract, and the check page marks each agent action as matching the rules or not.

4Built on NEAR

  • Strategy contract holds positions, enforces the public limits, tracks joiners and settles the fee split.
  • Access key lets the agent call only that contract, so a limit cannot be widened from outside.
  • NEAR Intents brings funds in and out from any chain.
  • NEAR's private AI for attested agent decisions being confirmed

5What we claim

We claim

  • Rules that are committed, private until close or until a key is bought, and checkable afterwards.

We do not claim

  • That the agent makes money, or hidden pool positions, or full anonymity.
  • That a viewing key cannot be copied or passed on.
