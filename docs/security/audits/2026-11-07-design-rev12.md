# Design audits of architecture revision 12 (e2b54a1)

Section 5 and the section 4 `holdings` row. Three fresh agents, scope only.

- **security-auditor: block** (Critical 1, High 5, Medium 8, Low 6).
  - C1: a lost add adopted in `Closing` goes into `positions` but not `close_snapshot`, so nothing can remove it once `Closed`.
  - H1: a gas-starved `ft_transfer_call` still makes the token refund, so the wallet balance can rise and fall around a reconcile read; EC-C02, C33 and C80 still require refunds.
  - H2: `resolve_stale` releases the lock while the stale promise can still run, and a reconcile in between counts tokens twice.
  - H3: a lost remove callback leaves the proceeds uncredited and misses its fees.
  - H4: settling a disputed claim from a balance read can pay twice or lock the residual.
  - H5: kept transfers and over-funding are locked in `Cancelled`.
  - M1 to M8: `match_unknown` forged with a dust gift; `add_in_flight` missing from holdings; swaps and adds strip value into residual; claims race reconcile; swaps and deposits sized from amounts not yet spendable; round-2 slot rule for rebalances; forced `E204` shown as not matching; one unpayable claim blocks both sweeps. L1 to L6 as listed in the report.
- **contract-reviewer: not ready** (High 1, Medium 9, Low 8).
  - H1: the swap output sits in `pending`, so the deposit right after the swap has nothing in `held` to spend; every open that needs a swap fails.
  - M1 to M9: swap sized beyond `held`; lost swap callback leaves `pending` too high; lost remove strands proceeds; adoption in `Closing` (as security C1); gift ties on `match_unknown`; storage drain after the run and by made-up token arguments; `MAX_CONTINUATIONS` unchecked against gas segments; `withdraw_storage_surplus` before `reveal`; a hostile agent can keep `close()` from running after `run_end`. L1 to L8 as listed.
