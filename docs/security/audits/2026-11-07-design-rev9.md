- **Scope:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` sections 4 and 5 at commit `41311b1`.
- **Auditors:** `security-auditor` (fresh) and `contract-reviewer` (fresh), each given only the scope.
- **Verdicts:** security-auditor **block** (1 Critical, 2 High, 7 Medium, 10 Low); contract-reviewer **not ready** (2 High, 8 Medium, 10 Low, 1 Info).
