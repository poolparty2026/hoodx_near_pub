Three fresh agents, scope only. Addressed in revision 24 (D49).

Both design audits found the same High: a call relayed through the caller's own contract let an outsider stall the off-chain receipt check (its own receipts in the walked tree) or make its transaction unfindable (delays past `TX_SCAN_BLOCKS`), hiding or blurring verdicts. That check had been the High finding in four rounds in a row. Erik chose to drop the receipt walk (D49).
