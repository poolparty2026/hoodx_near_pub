# Design audits of architecture revision 11 (90536a3)

Section 5 and the section 4 `holdings` row, with EC-C100 to EC-C109, EC-W20 and T59 to T64. Three fresh agents, scope only: `security-auditor` (block: Critical 1, High 3, Medium 7, Low 5), `contract-reviewer` (not ready: High 1, Medium 11, Low 9), `test-auditor` on the spike and edge cases (fix before merge: Medium 5, Low 9). All findings are addressed in revision 12 (D36).

## security-auditor

| ID | Severity | Finding | Resolution in revision 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Critical | Reconcile credited `held` from one wallet read that a refundable `ft_transfer_call` could inflate for a moment; a failed payout's real tokens then stay in DCL above `dcl_own`, unrecoverable, and the creator's claim fails forever | `ft_on_transfer` never refunds (every inbound transfer except valid funding is kept as residual), so no balance can rise and fall around a read; reconcile credits the internal surplus first; claims pay at most what the balance can cover; EC-C03, EC-C101 |
| H1 | High | `pending[out] += quote_out` and `pending += deposit - need` create amounts that never arrive | Every `pending` entry is exact or a lower bound: swaps expect `min_out` (EC-C90), add refunds are not expected and stay residual, deposits only `need - dcl_own` |
| H2 | High | A hostile agent can re-park a chain forever | `continue_deadline` fixed at the first park; at most 3 continuations; continuation needs gas for the whole stage; anyone aborts after `run_end + exit_grace`; EC-C110 |
| H3 | High | Amounts left "short" in the DCL internal balance can never be taken out | Every outgoing amount moves to `pending` before its promise, so reconcile finds it; `sweep_dcl_residual` after `Closed`; EC-C111 |
| M1 | Medium | `cleanup_gift` can take a lost add's position; gifts can be adopted | `match_unknown()` during the run adopts only exactly one match; `cleanup_gift` refuses positions matching an unresolved `Unknown` add; EC-C112 |
| M2 | Medium | One gift before `start` blocks it forever | `start` needs `MIN_FREE_SLOTS` free, not zero used; `cleanup_gift` allowed in `Open`; EC-C109 |
| M3 | Medium | A gift after round 1 makes the strategy pay for a swap with no add | Slots re-read before the swap |
| M4 | Medium | A stuck snapshot position blocks settlement forever | Stuck-position test at settle; `recover_position` after `Closed`; EC-C113 |
| M5 | Medium | `holdings` left out `pending`, so stop losses could latch on a bookkeeping gap | `holdings` counts `pending` (now never more than will land) |
| M6 | Medium | The contract can't tell `SwapOutOfGas` from a slippage refusal | The contract records `SwapRefused { used: 0, forwarded_gas }`; the check page classifies from the DCL receipt |
| M7 | Medium | EC-C03, EC-C90, EC-C101, EC-C108 and T51 contradicted the design | Rewritten to match revision 12 |
| L1 | Low | `resolve_stale` on a parked chain | Refused; parked chains end only through `abort_pending` |
| L2 | Low | Deposit amount and swap-sizing holdings undefined | Swap sized from `held + dcl_own + pending + predict_remove`; deposit `need - dcl_own` |
| L3 | Low | Fixed callback gas conflicted with "park if gas is short" | Per-stage `STAGE_GAS` and `CALLBACK_GAS`; a callback never starts a large stage itself |
| L4 | Low | `Aborted` had no verdict rule | Never excused (EC-W20) |
| L5 | Low | Pre-registration by a third party | Listed in 5.11 for the sandbox |
