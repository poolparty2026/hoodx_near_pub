# Design audit, 5 November 2026

- **Scope:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/gates/`, `docs/security/THREAT_MODEL.md`, `docs/security/EDGE_CASES.md`, judged against `docs/build-brief.md`.
- **Commit:** `78b51fa`. **Auditor:** `security-auditor`, a fresh instance, read-only.
- **No tests were run.** No production code exists yet, and the spike was not re-run.
- **Verdict:** **block.** Critical 3, High 6, Medium 10, Low 8.

Each finding has a status. **Revision 2** of the design (same day) addresses them; the re-audit decides whether each is resolved. Resolutions are recorded here and re-checked by a new auditor.

## Critical

| ID | Finding | Failure scenario | Proposed fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Slippage and price limits have no trusted reference: the contract relies on the agent's `observed` price and its `min_output_amount`. There are no per-action or per-day value caps | A stolen agent key moves the pool price, then swaps or adds at the manipulated price with a matching `observed` price. It repeats until the fund is drained | The contract reads its own reference price from Rhea and computes minimum outputs itself. Add `max_swap_amount`, `max_value_moved_per_day`, and a bound on the range centre against the reference | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| C2 | No exit anyone can trigger: only the creator can close, `settle` needs Rhea live, stuck Pending records block settlement, and there's no exit from `Open` | The creator is unavailable, Rhea is paused, a thin pool fails slippage, or a callback runs out of gas, and funds are locked | A public `run_end`, after which anyone can close and later exit. Cancel from `Open`. Settle in kind when a swap back isn't possible. Anyone can resolve a stale Pending record. Reserved callback gas | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| C3 | An outsider can stop `settle` from ever completing | Thousands of dust joins push `settle` over the gas limit, or dust of a non-quote token sent after close blocks "all in quote token" | Payouts worked out at claim time, minimum join size, joiner cap, dust threshold or settling in kind | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |

## High

| ID | Finding | Proposed fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | The full-access key on the strategy account (D9) can transfer funds directly, or swap code and swap it back, without the code-hash check noticing. The check page reads only the contract's own log | Delete the key at `start` with an in-contract recovery path, or use a timelocked multisig, or have the check page scan every transaction and key change on the account. Correct T2, T12 and the README | Addressed in revision 2 (lead decision D9 to D12). Awaiting re-audit |
| H2 | The contract accepts outside money (no joiner allowlist, no total cap), against the brief's rule of team money only | An on-chain `allowed_joiners` list and `max_total_principal`, checked in every status | Addressed in revision 2 (lead decision D9 to D12). Awaiting re-audit |
| H3 | Mid-run joins can be priced wrongly: NAV leaves out idle, in-flight and Rhea-internal balances, joins can interleave, and the caps are large compared with the fund | Refuse joins while an action is Pending, lock joins, define NAV fully, use the reference price, lower the caps, or drop mid-run joins | Addressed in revision 2 (lead decision D9 to D12). Awaiting re-audit |
| H4 | Viewing keys: payment is released on delivery, so a wrong key can't be refunded (contradicts EC-K03). The deliverer has no key of its own | A dispute window, or state plainly that buyers trust the deliverer. Give the deliverer a key limited to `deliver_key` | Addressed in revision 2 (lead decision D9 to D12). Awaiting re-audit |
| H5 | The "nothing was skipped" check can't see an agent that does nothing. No-action decision records aren't committed on-chain | The check page samples Rhea at fixed intervals itself, and/or the agent posts a hash chain of all decision records on-chain | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| H6 | Rhea messages could be chosen by the agent (route, `output_token`, `referral_id`, free-form msg) | The contract builds every Rhea message from typed, checked fields. Check every hop. `referral_id` fixed empty | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |

## Medium

| ID | Finding | Proposed fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | The check page's RPC defence is circular, and it reads the wrong block | Two independent RPC providers. Check at `observed.block` and at the execution block | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M2 | No pause, and no way to replace the agent | A creator `pause()`. An `exit_all` run by the creator or anyone with contract-computed minimums | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M3 | NEAR drained through oversized agent records or agent-triggered storage deposits. Escrow and storage NEAR are mixed | Size limits, allowed-token registration only, once each, a separate storage reserve | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M4 | Any incoming transfer is treated as a join, whatever its msg or sender, including routers | Require `msg == "join"`. Refuse self, DCL and router senders | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M5 | Decimal numbers in the rule format conflict with integer maths | Integer basis points only | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M6 | The split formula is defined two ways (by shares, and by principal plus fees) | One normative formula in `packages/rules`, tested against the contract | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M7 | If the creator never reveals, the check page can never verify anything | Anyone may call `reveal` (the hash check is the security). Show "unverifiable: not revealed" | Addressed in revision 2 (lead decision D9 to D12). Awaiting re-audit |
| M8 | Attestations aren't tied to an action, and can be cherry-picked or left out | Bind the strategy, action index and block in the request. Required when claimed. Hash-chain every request | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M9 | EC-C07 conflicts with the creator's timeout path. EC-A05 has no mechanism behind it | Fix EC-C07 and add EC-C42. Refuse a duplicate `decision_hash` (EC-C38) | Addressed in revision 2. Awaiting re-audit |
| M10 | The quote token issuer can pause and blacklist (unverified for USDC on NEAR) | Confirm with D3. Add T27, a README disclosure and claim retry | Partly addressed (T27, EC-C34, claim retry). Still to verify on-chain |

## Missing threats and edge cases

The auditor proposed threats T21 to T36, corrections to T2, T3, T8, T10, T12, T15, T19 and T20, and edge cases EC-C25 to EC-C44, EC-R09 to EC-R11, EC-A08 and EC-A09, EC-K04 and EC-K05, EC-W06 to EC-W11, and EC-S04. They are added to `THREAT_MODEL.md` and `EDGE_CASES.md` as part of the design revision.

## Unverified by the auditor

- Rhea DCL behaviour: liquidity arguments, whether positions can be transferred, how swap output is delivered, referral fees, storage per position.
- NRAS expiry.
- USDC pause and blacklist.
- Allowance top-up.
- The spike results, which were not re-run.
