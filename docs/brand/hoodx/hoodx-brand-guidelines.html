1What changes, and what does not

The product is the same: an autonomous LP agent on Rhea (NEAR) where creators run strategies with hidden rules, followers can buy a viewing key to read the rules during the run, and anyone can check at close that the agent followed them. The look stays broadly as it was in the October 2026 overview. The name, the wordmark and the mascot change.

Note on "poolparty". In the repository it appears only as the GitHub organisation (poolparty2026). The product is called Poolside in every document, so this guide renames the product: Poolside becomes hoodx. Renaming the organisation is a separate step for a co-lead.

2Name and voice

hoodx: lowercase, one word, read "hood-x". The hood is the privacy layer: it keeps your range to yourself, and it is your neighbourhood of followers. The x is the check at close and the cross-chain side.

DoDon't

Name not cleared. No trademark, domain, handle or existing-product check has been done for "hoodx". Poolside had the same open issue. Nobody registers handles or domains, or posts under the name, until a co-lead decides (D22). A name search is step 1 in section 7.

Voice (unchanged)

  • Short, plain, exact. "One tap to zap in or join a strategy. The agent sets and manages the range."
  • Numbers before adjectives. Write exact amounts, never "a fair split".
  • One action per screen, with the verb on the button: Zap, Join, Manage, Close, Create.
  • Speak to "you" and "your LP agent". People are never "users".
  • Same word for the same thing: zap, range, rebalance, guardrail, strategy, creator, joiner, follower, viewing key, check page, close, split.

Taglines to test

Hackathon line: "Follow a private LP strategy, and prove the agent did what it promised."

Short lines: "Stay in the hood." · "Private until close. Checkable after." · "Your LP agent. Private by default."

Any line with "win" is paired with public profit and loss, never a promised return.

3Wordmark and mascot

The wordmark is hood in Helvetica Bold, navy, lowercase, tight, followed by a mint rounded chip holding a navy x. The mascot, Hoody, is Pooly's drop wearing a navy hood, with the face showing in the opening. Hoody is also the logo mark.

Rules

RuleValue
WordmarkNever capitals, italic, or a different font. The x chip stays mint with a navy outline on cream and on navy.
Clear spaceThe height of the x chip on all sides.
Minimum sizeLockup 28 mm / 110 px wide. Below that use the wordmark alone, or Hoody alone at 8 mm / 32 px.
On darkCream "hood", same mint chip. Hoody gets the 2 px cream halo, as Pooly did, because its hood is navy.
Hoody's shapeThe Pooly drop with a curled tip, a thick navy outline, a navy hood over the upper body with a faint lavender rim round the face opening, two side nubs, two feet, a soft belly with the [•] mark.
NeverStretch or recolour the hood, give Hoody a mood colour outside the table in section 5, or put the pastel x chip on a pastel background.

Files in this folder: hoodx-wordmark.svg, hoodx-wordmark-on-dark.svg, hoody-*.svg. The wordmark SVG uses live text in Helvetica, with Arial as a fallback: convert it to outlines before print or sending to a third party. Draft artwork, not a commissioned logo.

Rules

  • Navy is the only ink. No pure black, no grey borders. Pastels are fills, never text on cream.
  • Sequences run mint, lavender, peach, butter. Sky and pink are extras for illustration.
  • Dark app: background #1C1D3D, cards navy, text cream. Pastels may carry text there: mint for "matching", pink for "not matching", butter for warnings.
  • Type: Helvetica only. Headings bold, navy, sentence case. Eyebrows bold capitals in muted. Bold oblique for one pull quote. Tabular figures in the app, exact amounts always.
  • Shapes: 2 px navy outlines, 10 to 12 px radius, fully rounded pills. Tables have a mint header, white and lavender-tint rows. Covers use waves, sparkles and bubbles, and the app never does.

5Hoody and the six moods

Hoody takes Pooly's job. Each mood has a set body colour, and the hood stays navy. Use the mood that matches the state shown.

HappyDefault, success, strategy live
WowNew, reveal, rules unlocked
ShhPrivate, hidden rules, viewing key locked
HypeFees earned, a strategy closed in profit, launch
SleepyIdle, out of range, waiting, empty state
Cute-rektErrors, losses, refusals, not matching

Rules

  • Shh: a closed-eye face, a finger on the lips and a padlock on the belly. It is the privacy sign. Use it wherever something is hidden until close or until a viewing key is bought.
  • One Hoody per screen. Friend characters (smaller drops in sky, lavender with a bow, peach with a crown) are cover art only and never replace Hoody.
  • Never use Hype next to a profit claim. Cute-rekt always shows the real number.
  • On navy, Hoody wears the cream halo (hoody-on-dark-happy.svg).

These limits come from CLAUDE.md and the build brief. They apply to every page, post, screen and video under the new name.

Also: no Shade Agents. Mention "formerly Poolside" only where a co-lead decides it helps. Every new text goes through the claims-checker before it ships.

Checklist before shipping anything visual

  1. Navy ink only; pastels only as fills (or as text on dark).
  2. The fee split is mint, lavender and peach.
  3. Sequences follow mint, lavender, peach, butter.
  4. Hoody's mood matches the state; Shh marks anything private.
  5. Rounded shapes with 2 px navy outlines.
  6. No profit, anonymity, hidden-position or "unstoppable" claim.
  7. Sentence-case headings, "hoodx" lowercase, Helvetica only.
